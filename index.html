Questions, answered

How much does a website cost?

Landing pages start at ₱5,000, business websites at ₱10,000 and custom development at ₱20,000. The final price depends on your pages, content and features, and we confirm it in a quote before work begins.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size of the site and how quickly content and feedback come in. We give you an estimated timeline in your quote.

What information do you need from me?

Your business name and goals, the pages you want, your text and photos (or help sourcing them), your logo and colours if you have them, and a few websites you like.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Yes. Every website is designed to be responsive and is checked on phone, tablet and desktop screen sizes.

Can you redesign an existing website?

Yes. Send us the current address and tell us what is not working, and we will suggest a redesign that fits your goals.

Do I need to purchase a domain and hosting?

Most websites need both. These are separate costs from the design fee. We can advise on options and help set them up.

Can I request changes after launch?

Yes. Small fixes can be arranged after handover, and larger updates can be quoted separately or covered by a maintenance plan. The agreed scope is set out before the project starts.

Do you provide ongoing maintenance?

Yes, as an optional service starting at ₱1,000 per month. We will agree what it covers before it begins.
